Scribatic records a conversation and turns it into a transcript on your iPhone or iPad, marking who said what. Nothing is uploaded: the app makes no network requests of its own, so your recordings stay on the device.

What you can do
• Record a meeting, lecture, interview or call, and watch the words appear as people speak
• Transcribe in about 99 languages: Scribatic recognises the language from the first words, or you can choose one
• Pause and resume, then stop to save the note
• See each speaker's words marked in their own colour, identified automatically when you stop
• Give speakers names, only on this device and only for that note
• Play a recording back, or identify the speakers again if the count was off
• Share a transcript to any app you choose, with or without the speakers' names
• Delete the audio and keep the words, or delete a note entirely
• Find notes grouped by day, in light or dark appearance

Private by design
• Transcription and speaker identification run on the device's own processor
• Recordings, transcripts and names are kept in the app's private storage and left out of iCloud and device backups
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting
• When you share a transcript, it goes only to the app you pick, and that app sends it

Good to know
• The speech models download once through the App Store when you first open the app, about 205 MB
• Accuracy depends on the recording: clear speech close to the device works best

Scribatic is built on the open-source whisper.cpp and sherpa-onnx projects. Its source code is public at github.com/niroshpg/scribatic.
