Scribatic transcribes speech entirely on the device. There is no account, no login and no server; the app makes no network requests of its own.

On first launch the Models screen appears while the speech models download as Apple-hosted Background Assets (about 470 MB). Tap Continue once they show Installed.

To try it: tap the orange microphone button on the Notes screen, which starts recording at once. Speak for a few seconds (or play a conversation near the device), then tap Stop. The note opens with the transcript; speakers are identified when the recording stops. Tap a speaker's name to rename them. Share is in the toolbar; delete options are under More.

To correct text, touch and hold a part of the transcript and choose Edit text; Play this part plays its audio. Import audio is under the Notes screen's More (…) menu. Storage & memory and Backup are on the Models screen (the chip icon on the Notes screen). A backup is written only to a folder the user picks in the Files picker, encrypted with a passphrase they choose (AES-256-GCM through Apple's CryptoKit); the app never uploads it.

Microphone access is used only while recording. Transcription is designed for English; other languages are offered as experimental. The language is detected from the first words, or can be chosen under Models > Transcription.

Scribatic Pro (in-app purchase scribatic_pro, non-consumable) adds summaries, search across notes and Ask your notes. Its model needs 8 GB of memory, so Pro is offered only on devices with 8 GB or more (for example iPhone 15 Pro and later, or an M-series iPad); on a device with less, the purchase screen says Pro isn't offered there. To reach it: open a note, then tap Summarise under Summary, or tap the search icon on the Notes screen. After purchase, Pro's model (2.5 GB) downloads once as an Apple-hosted Background Asset.
