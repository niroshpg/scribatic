Scribatic records a conversation and turns it into a transcript on your phone, marking who said what. Nothing is uploaded: the app has no internet permission, so it has no way to send your recordings anywhere.

What you can do
• Record a meeting, lecture, interview or call, and watch the words appear as people speak
• Pause and resume, then stop to save the note
• See each speaker's words marked in their own colour, identified automatically when you stop
• Give speakers names, only on this device and only for that note
• Play a recording back, or identify the speakers again if the count was off
• Share a transcript to any app you choose, with or without the speakers' names
• Delete the audio and keep the words, or delete a note entirely
• Find notes grouped by day, with light and dark themes

Private by design
• Transcription and speaker identification run on the phone's own processor
• The app has no INTERNET permission, so recordings, transcripts and names cannot leave the phone through it
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting
• When you share a transcript, it goes only to the app you pick, and that app sends it

Good to know
• Transcription is in English
• The speech models download once through Google Play when you first open the app: about 205 MB is required, plus an optional 1.3 GB model you can leave out on the setup screen
• Accuracy depends on the recording: clear speech close to the phone works best
• Needs a 64-bit (ARM64) Android phone running Android 9 or later

Scribatic is built on the open-source whisper.cpp and sherpa-onnx projects. Its source code is public at github.com/niroshpg/scribatic.
