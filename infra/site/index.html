Scribatic

On-device speech & recall

Your recordings, transcribed and searchable, without ever leaving your phone.

Scribatic transcribes speech and answers questions about your own recordings using whisper.cpp and llama.cpp, running directly on the device's own compute.

Coming soon for iOS and Android.

  • 01 Runs entirely on-device. Transcription and question answering happen on the device itself, so no server ever sees your audio.
  • 02 No network path to leave by. The Android build ships without the INTERNET permission; the iOS build contains no URLSession call site.
  • 03 No account, no telemetry. Nothing to sign up for, nothing being measured or reported back.
  • 04 Native on both platforms. Independent SwiftUI and Jetpack Compose apps sharing one C++ engine core.
  • 05 No hardware gate. Vendor assistants need recent silicon and an account. This needs an ARM64 phone.

Following along

There is no waitlist and no signup form here, because there is nothing on this page that collects anything about you. The repository is the most reliable signal that something has shipped — watch or star it on GitHub. If you would rather just say something, info@scribatic.com reaches a person.
