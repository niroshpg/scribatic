Scribatic

Privacy policy

Scribatic collects nothing.

Your recordings, transcripts and speaker names are created and kept on your device. The app has no way to send them anywhere.

Last updated 30 September 2026. Applies to the Scribatic apps for iOS and Android.

What stays on your device

  • Audio recordings you make in the app.
  • Transcripts, produced on the device by speech models that run on its own processor.
  • Speaker labels and names you give them. Telling voices apart happens on the device, and nothing about a voice is kept beyond the labels in each note.

These are stored inside the app's private storage and are excluded from device and cloud backups. Deleting a recording or a note removes it from the device. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no server of ours that the app talks to. The Android app does not have the internet permission, and the iOS app makes no network requests of its own.

The microphone

The app asks for microphone access so it can record. The microphone is open only while you are recording, and the audio goes nowhere but the device.

Model downloads

The speech models are delivered by the App Store or Google Play, the same way the app itself is. Apple and Google handle those downloads under their own privacy policies; no request goes to us. If you choose to install the models from files instead, you download them yourself in your browser from the project's public release page.

Sharing

When you share a transcript, it is handed to the app you choose in the system share sheet, and that app sends it. You can share without speaker names. Nothing is shared unless you do it.

Information Apple and Google may give us

If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple or Google may show us aggregated statistics and crash reports in their developer consoles. They do not include your recordings or transcripts, and the app itself sends nothing.

Children

Scribatic collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: info@scribatic.com. The app's source code is public at github.com/niroshpg/scribatic, so these statements can be checked.
